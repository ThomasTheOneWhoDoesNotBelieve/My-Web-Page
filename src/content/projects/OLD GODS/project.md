note: "**ASAKUSA:** I don't like looking at the main stage. There is rarely anything interesting there. Where the light shines, the lies and effort go—but what about the places we don't look at? What about the places we only catch from the corners of our eyes—the places forgotten by people, time, and nature? **UENO:** The main stage is the gratification of our expectations. We need to contain entertainment behind bars to ensure that the main stage remains in front of us. Having guidance on where to look is not a limitation; it is the road that allows us to walk and wonder."
